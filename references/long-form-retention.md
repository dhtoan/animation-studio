# Long-form retention (honest techniques)

## First 30 seconds
Show the stakes before any context: a number, a contrast, or an image of the outcome. Skip the logo sting and the "welcome back".

## The promise
Early on, say what the viewer will be able to understand or do by the end. Then deliver it.

## Open loops
Only open a loop you will close. Every question raised at the start of a chapter must be answered later in the video.

## Pattern changes
- Switch engine, camera move or visual mode at each chapter boundary, and roughly every 20–40 s.
- The motion must explain something (draw, build, compare). It is not decoration.

## Evidence and pacing
- **Visual proof for every claim:** a chart, a document, a diagram, or a dramatisation labelled as one.
- **Re-hook at each chapter start:** one sentence recapping where we are, then the new question.
- **Pacing:**
  - Keep scenes to 8–25 s.
  - Vary sentence length.
  - Leave 0.3–0.6 s breathing pads (scene `pad`).

## Ending
Give the answer, then the takeaway tool, then one natural bridge to a related video. Skip "smash that like button" filler.

## What we don't do
- Fake cliffhangers.
- Invented drama.
- Misleading thumbnails or titles.
- Withholding the answer to pad the runtime.
